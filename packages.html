Questions

Good to know

Anything else on your mind? Send me a message.

When is the best time for a maternity shoot?

Most mums book between 28 and 34 weeks, when the bump is beautifully round and you still feel comfortable moving around.

When should we book a newborn session?

The first two weeks are ideal for sleepy, curled-up newborn photos, so it's best to get in touch before your due date and we'll pencil in a time.

Where do sessions take place?

Anywhere that feels like you — a park, woodland, the beach or your own home. I'm happy to suggest locations with lovely light.

What should we wear?

Soft, coordinating colours work best. Flowing gowns and traditional outfits look stunning too. Once you book, I'll send you a few ideas.

Can we bring the dog?

Of course. Pets are part of the family, and some of my favourite photos have a dog in them.

How do I book?

Send an enquiry from the contact page with your preferred dates, and I'll get back to you to confirm the details.

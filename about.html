About Vaishnavi

The person behind the camera at Vassu's Photography.

From a hobby to the part of my week I love most

I'm Vaishnavi Pawar, a photographer who fell in love with capturing families. What began as a hobby has become the thing I look forward to most: meeting new families, hearing their story, and turning an ordinary afternoon into photographs they'll treasure for years.

My style is natural and light-filled, with gentle guidance rather than stiff poses. I love golden autumn leaves, soft window light, traditional sarees and flowing gowns alike — and I'm always happy to include grandparents, siblings and the family dog.

  • Relaxed, gently guided sessions — no experience in front of a camera needed
  • Outdoor locations or the comfort of your own home
  • Help choosing outfits, colours and props
  • Carefully edited photos delivered in a private online gallery
